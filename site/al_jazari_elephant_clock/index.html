Elephant Clock

Fī maʿrifat al-ḥiyal al-handasiyya (The Book of Knowledge of Ingenious Mechanical Devices) - Elephant Clock

Autor: Ismail al-Jazari

Obra: Kitāb fī maʿrifat al-ḥiyal al-handasiyya (1206, 1206)

Páginas/Láminas: Category I, Chapter 4

Nivel de Reconstrucción: B

Dominio Físico: Hydraulics, Kinematics, Statics

Descripción

An elaborate water clock shaped like an Asian elephant. It uses a sinking bowl with a calibrated orifice floating in a water tank hidden inside the elephant. As the bowl fills and sinks, it triggers strings and pulleys that release balls, strike a cymbal, and move automata (a scribe, an eagle, a mahout) to mark the passage of a half-hour.
