Twin-Cylinder Suction Pump

Water-raising machine with twin cylinders

Autor: Ismail al-Jazari

Obra: Kitāb fī maʿrifat al-ḥiyal al-handasiyya (1206, 1206)

Páginas/Láminas: Category V, Chapter 5

Nivel de Reconstrucción: B

Dominio Físico: Hydraulics, Kinematics

Descripción

A water-raising machine featuring an early use of a true crank and a slot-rod mechanism. A paddle wheel turned by a stream drives a horizontal gear wheel, which has a peg moving in a slotted rod. The rod pulls and pushes two opposed pistons in copper cylinders, drawing water through flap valves and pumping it up to a higher elevation.
