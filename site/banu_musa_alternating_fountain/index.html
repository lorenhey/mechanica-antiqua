Alternating Fountain

Fountain that changes its shape

Autor: Banū Mūsā

Obra: Kitāb al-Ḥiyal (The Book of Ingenious Devices) (850, c. 850)

Páginas/Láminas: Model 91

Nivel de Reconstrucción: B

Dominio Físico: Hydraulics, Kinematics

Descripción

A fountain that alternates its spray pattern between a single straight vertical jet and a wide shield-like spray. It achieves this using a clever hidden mechanism with a water-driven balance beam (like a tipping bucket) that directs the water flow into alternating pipes.

Nivel de Confianza

High
