Automatic Flute Player

The Instrument which Plays by Itself

Autor: Banū Mūsā

Obra: Kitāb al-Ḥiyal (The Book of Ingenious Devices) (850, c. 850)

Páginas/Láminas: Model 100

Nivel de Reconstrucción: C

Dominio Físico: Pneumatics, Kinematics

Descripción

Considered one of the earliest programmable machines. It consists of a large mechanical flute player. Water flow drives a waterwheel, turning a large drum with pins on it. The pins trip levers that close and open the holes of a flute. A continuous air supply is generated by two alternating water vessels that compress air into the flute.

Nivel de Confianza

Medium
